Vegetable Biryani

Description:

Vegetable biryani is a fragrant rice dish made by cooking basmati rice with mixed vegetables, aromatic spices, herbs, and saffron or turmeric. The rice absorbs the flavors of the spices while remaining light and fluffy. It makes a satisfying main course and is commonly served with raita, pickle, or a simple salad.

    Ingredients:

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables — carrots, peas, beans, and cauliflower
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 2 green chilies, slit
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tablespoons oil or ghee
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1-inch cinnamon stick
  • 3–4 cloves
  • 2–3 cardamom pods
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 3½ cups water
  • Salt, to taste
  • Fresh coriander
  • Fresh mint
  • Optional: saffron soaked in warm milk

    Steps:

  1. Wash the basmati rice several times until the water becomes relatively clear. Soak it for about 20–30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil or ghee in a large pot. Add cumin, bay leaf, cinnamon, cloves, and cardamom. Fry briefly until fragrant.
  3. Add sliced onion and cook until lightly golden.
  4. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilies. Cook for another minute.
  5. Add the chopped tomato and mixed vegetables. Cook until the vegetables begin to soften.
  6. Add turmeric, chili powder, garam masala, and salt. Stir well.
  7. Drain the soaked rice and add it to the pot. Gently mix it with the vegetables and spices.
  8. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil.
  9. Reduce the heat, cover the pot, and cook until the rice is tender and the water has been absorbed.
  10. Turn off the heat and let the biryani rest, covered, for about 5–10 minutes.
  11. Fluff the rice gently with a fork and garnish with fresh coriander and mint.
  12. Serve hot with raita or your preferred side dish.
