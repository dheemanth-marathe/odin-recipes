Masala Dosa

Description:

Masala dosa is a classic South Indian dish consisting of a thin, crispy fermented rice-and-lentil crepe filled with a savory potato masala. It is traditionally served hot with coconut chutney and sambar. The combination of the crisp dosa, soft spiced potatoes, and tangy accompaniments makes it a popular breakfast or light meal.

Ingredients:

    For the dosa:

  • 2 cups rice
  • ½ cup urad dal (split black gram)
  • ½ teaspoon fenugreek seeds
  • Salt, to taste
  • Water, as needed
  • Oil or ghee, for cooking

    For the potato filling:

  • 4 medium potatoes
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 1–2 green chilies, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1 teaspoon chana dal
  • 8–10 curry leaves
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Salt, to taste
  • Fresh coriander, chopped

    Steps:

  1. Prepare the batter: Wash and soak the rice, urad dal, and fenugreek seeds separately for about 4–6 hours.
  2. Drain and grind them with water into a smooth, moderately thick batter.
  3. Add salt, cover the batter, and leave it in a warm place overnight to ferment.
  4. Prepare the filling: Boil the potatoes until tender, peel them, and break them into small pieces.
  5. Heat oil in a pan. Add mustard seeds and chana dal. Once they begin to crackle, add curry leaves, green chilies, and onion.
  6. Cook until the onion softens. Add turmeric, salt, and the potatoes. Mix well and cook for a few minutes.
  7. Make the dosa: Heat a lightly greased tawa or flat pan. Pour a ladleful of batter into the center and spread it outward in a thin circle.
  8. Drizzle a little oil or ghee around the edges and cook until the dosa becomes golden and crisp.
  9. Place some potato filling in the center, fold the dosa, and serve hot with sambar and chutney.
