Paneer Butter Masala

Description

Paneer butter masala is a rich North Indian curry made with cubes of paneer simmered in a smooth tomato-based gravy. Butter, cream, and aromatic spices give the sauce its characteristic creamy texture and mildly sweet, savory flavor. It is commonly served with naan, roti, or steamed rice.

Ingredients:

    For the paneer:

  • 250 g paneer, cut into cubes
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • A pinch of salt
  • ½ teaspoon chili powder

    For the gravy:

  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 large tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 10–12 cashews
  • ½ teaspoon cumin seeds
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • ½ teaspoon coriander powder
  • ½ cup water
  • ¼ cup fresh cream
  • Salt, to taste
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

    Steps:

  1. Cut the paneer into bite-sized cubes. Lightly season with salt and chili powder.
  2. Heat a little oil in a pan and lightly fry the paneer until the outside develops a little color. Remove and set aside.
  3. In the same pan, add a little butter. Add cumin seeds and let them sizzle.
  4. Add onion and cook until softened.
  5. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for another minute.
  6. Add chopped tomatoes and cashews. Cook until the tomatoes become soft.
  7. Allow the mixture to cool slightly, then blend it into a smooth paste.
  8. Return the paste to the pan. Add turmeric, chili powder, coriander powder, garam masala, salt, and water.
  9. Simmer the gravy for several minutes until it thickens and becomes glossy.
  10. Add the fried paneer cubes and gently stir them into the gravy.
  11. Reduce the heat and add the cream. Simmer for another 2–3 minutes.
  12. Garnish with fresh coriander and a small swirl of cream or butter.
  13. Serve hot with naan, roti, or rice.
